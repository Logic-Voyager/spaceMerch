THE ENIGMA STORY

It started with a question.

What if the feeling of looking up at the night sky could travel with you?

Enigma began during a five-hour SpaceCon hackathon with a small idea and a very large subject: make astronomy feel closer, more welcoming, and more fun to explore.

Today, we turn that feeling into fictional space-themed goods for observers, dreamers, and anyone still curious about what lies beyond the next horizon.
